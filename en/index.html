    What is AstroLabs?
    A studio and a Windows launcher: games built for TikTok lives, where the audience acts on the game in real time.
    Do I need an account?
    Yes, a free AstroLabs account. It is the same on the website and in the launcher: a game bought here is waiting for you in the launcher.
    How do I pay?
    By card, through Stripe: AstroLabs never sees your bank details. You can also buy from the launcher.
