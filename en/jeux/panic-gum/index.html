How it works on your live

The game runs on your PC while you are live. It listens to what happens on your live and turns it into events on the square.

  1. Pick your source

    In the game’s TikTok tab: EulerStream with your username and API key, or Tik360 / TikFinity on the same computer. Both start on their own.

  2. Set up your gifts

    Link each TikTok gift to one or more actions, with the amount and duration you want. Click TEST to try it without waiting for the live.

  3. Go live

    Your viewers send, the square reacts. The sender’s name shows up with the action they trigger.

  • GiftsEach gift runs the action you choose. A ×N combo gives the action N times, even when several viewers send at once.
  • Like barAt each step, the chosen action fires, or the like wheel spins and draws one.
  • TappersWhen one viewer reaches the like threshold you set (150 by default), their action fires.
  • New followerA drone brings their name in glitter balls and drops it on the square.
  • Keyboard shortcutsEach gift or event can get its own key, so you can fire it yourself during a game.
  • Nothing gets lostHundreds of gifts at once? Every send becomes an action, run one after the other.

Panic Gum questions

Do I need EulerStream?
No. You pick your source in the game’s TikTok tab: EulerStream (TikTok username and API key), or Tik360 / TikFinity running on the same computer, with the webhook links the game gives you.
Can I try the actions without being live?
Yes: each gift has a TEST button that runs all its actions, and you can also give them a keyboard shortcut.
What if hundreds of gifts arrive at once?
Every send becomes an action, run one after the other: nothing is refused or lost. ×N combos give the action N times.
Are updates paid?
No. They come through the launcher, and the list of changes shows up in the game after each update.
Windows shows a warning when installing
That is normal for recent software: click “More info”, then “Run anyway”.
