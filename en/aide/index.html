A question? We’ve got you.

The launcher

Is the launcher free?
Yes. The launcher is free: you only pay for the games you choose. Download the launcher.
What computer does it run on?
A 64-bit Windows 10 or 11 PC, with an Internet connection.
Windows shows “Windows protected your PC”
That’s normal for new software. Click “More info”, then “Run anyway”.
How do I update the launcher and my games?
Nothing to do: the launcher updates itself and offers updates for your games.
The download fails
Our files are hosted by GitHub. If they have an incident, a banner appears on the download page: try again a few minutes later.

Account and purchases

Is my site account the same as my launcher account?
Yes, it’s the same AstroLabs account. A game bought on the site shows up in the launcher as soon as you sign in.
How do I buy a game?
On its page, click “Buy”: payment happens on a secure Stripe page. You can also buy right in the launcher. See the games.
I paid but the game doesn’t show up
Confirmation usually takes a few seconds. Make sure you’re signed in with the same account, then restart the launcher. If the game still doesn’t show up, email us with your account’s address.
I didn’t get the verification code
Check your spam folder. You can ask for a new code from the verification screen (once a minute). The code is valid for 15 minutes.
I forgot my password
Use your recovery code (16 characters, given when you created your account) under “Forgot password” on the account page. You’ll get a new recovery code.
I lost my recovery code
Email us from your account’s address: we’ll check it’s yours before helping.

TikTok live

How does the game react to my live?
Your viewers’ gifts, likes and follows trigger events in the game, live. Settings are in the game.
Which live source should I pick?
The game receives your live in one of two ways, in the TIKTOK tab of its settings:
  • EulerStream: direct connection with your TikTok username and a free key. Nothing else to install.
  • Tik360 or TikFinity: if you already use one of them, running on the same PC as the game.
One source at a time: connecting to EulerStream stops Tik360 reception.
How do I get my EulerStream key?
The key is free and takes a minute:
  1. Go to eulerstream.com, create an account or sign in, then open the Dashboard.
  2. On the Euler Stream API page, under API Keys, click Create your first key (or Create Key if you already have one).
  3. Give it a name, for example “Panic Gum”, then click Create API Key.
  4. Click Click to Copy API Key: the key is copied, and “Copied the API key to your clipboard” confirms it.
Where do I put the EulerStream key in the game?
  1. In the game menu, open SETTINGS, then the TIKTOK tab.
  2. Pick EULERSTREAM.
  3. In TIKTOK USERNAME, type your username without the @.
  4. Click in EULERSTREAM KEY and paste your key (Ctrl + V).
  5. Click CONNECT.
The key is stored encrypted on your PC: you only enter it once. After that, just start your live.
The game stays “Disconnected”
  • Your TikTok live must be running: the game receives nothing until you are live.
  • Check your username: no @, no spaces, exactly as on TikTok.
  • Paste your key again: click in the field, paste it, then click CONNECT.
  • Still nothing? On eulerstream.com, delete the key (Delete), create a new one and paste it in the game.
Is EulerStream paid?
Not for streaming: the free Community plan is enough for your lives. Its limits (connections per day) are shown under Rate Limits on your EulerStream dashboard.
Is my EulerStream key safe?
It stays encrypted on your PC and is only sent to EulerStream. Never share it, not even on Discord: AstroLabs will never ask for it. If you think it leaked, delete it on eulerstream.com (Delete) and create a new one.
Using Tik360 or TikFinity
  1. Run Tik360 or TikFinity on the same PC as the game, connected to your TikTok live.
  2. In the game, open SETTINGS, TIKTOK tab, then pick TIK360.
  3. Import the profile made for the game, or paste the links the game gives you into your “Webhook” reactions.
Your viewers’ username and avatar are read straight from Tik360 and TikFinity.
Are the games linked to TikTok?
No: AstroLabs is not affiliated with TikTok. Live interactions go through EulerStream, Tik360 or TikFinity.
